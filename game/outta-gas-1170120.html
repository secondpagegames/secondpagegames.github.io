OUTTA GAS

Trevor Kellogg · 2020-03-26 · 16.79 €

OUTTA GAS is a post-apocalyptic RPG built almost entirely by one developer, Trevor Kellogg, who coded, animated, and voiced the whole thing. You play Full Metal Jack, blasting apart monsters tile by tile to disable their eyes, legs, and claws in a turn-based shooting gallery combat system. With over 25 weapons and 70 unique monsters, reviewers call it a mashup of Mad Max and Fallout with genuine comedic writing. 96% positive across 51 reviews.

OUTTA GAS is a post-apocalyptic RPG featuring a turn-based SHOOTING GALLERY combat mechanic where blasting away body tiles damages monster health and decreases their combat stats. Explore four unique acts to find gas and get back on the road to the Party at the End of the World!
