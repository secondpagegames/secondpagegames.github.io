Roar of Revenge

SEEP · 2021-10-04 · 4.99 €

Roar of Revenge casts you as Keel the Barbarian, gathering relics to grow stronger before confronting the man-lion Leomhann. Built on NES-era pixel art and chiptune sound, it plays like a lost cartridge from the Rastan and Faxanadu era. Its sharpest idea is simple: the game tracks every death, and that count determines which of its multiple endings you unlock. Forgiving enough to finish, tough enough to master.

A game that puts together Rastan's action with adventure elements inspired by Faxanadu. An old school tribute for every lover of 8-bit classics!
