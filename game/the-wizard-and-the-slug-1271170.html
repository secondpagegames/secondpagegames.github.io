The Wizard and The Slug

Silkworm · 2020-11-02 · 4.99 €

The Wizard and The Slug ties offense and defense to a single shrinking resource: every attack or platforming trick reduces your slug's size, cutting cooldowns but raising the stakes if you're hit. Built around this one clever gimmick, the game wraps a three-act story with multiple endings, tight platforming, and boss fights that punch above their weight. Reviewers keep calling out the writing, comparing it to Undertale, and noting the whole thing came from a single developer.

Jump, dash, and shoot your way through this story-rich action 2D platformer. Together with your magical wizard companion, play as the world's cutest, fastest, most agile slug as you explore a world full of charm, humor, and endearing characters.
