Tales of the Tiny Planet

Pixelsplit · 2017-08-03 · 0.97 €

Tales of the Tiny Planet is a physics-puzzle adventure controlled entirely with one button. You guide small rolling planets through six worlds and seventy-two levels, timing precise presses to steer them through portals. It starts calm and cute, then turns genuinely difficult, with players citing worlds five and six as brutal tests of timing. With local co-op, a leaderboard, and a soundtrack players keep praising, it's a small, tightly built game that slipped past almost everyone.

Tales of the Tiny Planet tells the story of a separated group of planet friends who want to reunite. You have to assist them against the dangers of time and space.
