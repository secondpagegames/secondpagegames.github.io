Calturin

Lasse Zacho Malver · 2021-08-24 · 3.99 €

Calturin strips boss rush games down to the essentials: eight distinct bullet-hell boss fights, no filler enemies, and a dark fantasy story told through fully voiced characters. You're resurrected by a necromancer and forced to fight for your freedom through the Gauntlet of Portals, with red and black portals waiting for players who want an even harder version of each battle. With 89% positive reviews from just 56 players, it's a tightly crafted game that's flown almost entirely under the radar.

Calturin is a Bullet Hell Boss Rush game where you are resurrected by a necromancer who now holds you in his power. Promised your freedom if you do his bidding, you must traverse the Gauntlet of Portals to unknown lands and face deadly beings.
