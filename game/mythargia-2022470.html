Mythargia

RunByCoffee · 2023-02-20 · 5.99 €

Mythargia casts you as a journalist chasing a lead to an abandoned island tied to two dimensions. Instead of combat or action-driven progression, the game centers on a build-your-own-story mechanic: gathering journal entries and photos to piece together the article yourself. Reviewers praised this as genuinely original, likening it to Frogwares' investigative titles. A short, atmospheric pixel-art mystery for players who want their curiosity to do the heavy lifting.

You are a journalist in search of your breakthrough story. A promising lead takes you to the abandoned island of Mythargia where you have to discover its buried history connecting two different dimensions.
