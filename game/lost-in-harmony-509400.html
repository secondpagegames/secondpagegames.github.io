Lost in Harmony

Digixart · 2018-06-21 · 6.99 €

Before Road 96, there was Kaito's first adventure. Lost in Harmony, from DigixArt, is a musical runner where rhythmic tapping and dream-like storytelling collide. Players guide Kaito through his backstory with Aya, set to a soundtrack from composers including Wyclef Jean and Borislav Slavov. Reviewers describe it as beautiful, mysterious, and emotionally heavy, with a steep but rewarding learning curve. If Road 96 left you curious about its characters, this is where the story began.

The first game from DigixArt! Dive into a fantastic musical odyssey, brought by a wonderful soundtrack imagined by renowned composers.
