Pathfinders: Memories

Mens Sana Interactive · 2021-02-16 · 2.99 €

Pathfinders: Memories asks a simple but sharp question: how good is your memory, really? You study a level's obstacles and layout, then the path disappears, leaving you to trace it from recall alone. Checkpoints help, but using them costs points, so purists play blind. Set across six ancient civilizations and sixty levels of rising difficulty, it's a cheap, focused puzzle experience with charming art and a genuinely different hook.

Memory is the key. Use it to help Pathfinders travel through time visiting ancient human civilizations and search for relics. The path to success is full of entrapments, obstacles, and enemies. Memorize them and draw the safe path for our heroes.
