Chronescher

PurpleSloth · 2022-08-31 · 3.99 €

Chronescher drops you into an Escher-esque world where geometry bends and nothing stays where you left it. Place portals, snapshot the level with time anchors, and shift perspective to uncover paths that shouldn't exist. Spread across six biomes, each introducing new mechanics, it builds toward a quiet mystery about who you're actually playing. Reviewers compare it favorably to Monument Valley and Manifold Garden, calling out the low price for the amount of content. With only 58 reviews, it's a puzzle game that hasn't found its audience yet.

Chronescher is a challenging turn-based puzzle game in Escher-esque spaces. Use impossible geometries, teleportation and environmental snapshots to navigate your mind and uncover your lost memories.
